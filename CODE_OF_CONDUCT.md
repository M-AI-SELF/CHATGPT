# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We aim to make participation in M-AI-SELF :: Ω Runtime welcoming, respectful, constructive, and safe for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Assume good faith while remaining open to correction.
- Give actionable feedback and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Respect privacy, consent, ownership, and differing capabilities.
- Help keep technical discussions factual and focused.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, doxxing, threats, deliberate disruption, and sharing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. Reports should be made privately to the repository maintainers. Maintainers should investigate fairly, protect reporter privacy, and take proportionate action.

This document is an initial project policy and may be refined as the contributor community develops.
